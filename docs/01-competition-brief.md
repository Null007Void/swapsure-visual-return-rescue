# Competition brief
SwapSure targets the YouCam API Skin AI & eCommerce VTO Hackathon, sponsored by Perfect Mobile Corp. The correct round is the eCommerce event, not the earlier Apparel event.

## Confirmed event requirements
The deadline is November 2, 2026 at 11:45 AM EST, or 9:45 AM in Arizona. A working application must integrate at least one YouCam API and demonstrate consumer or ecommerce value. Provide source/assets/run instructions through a public licensed repository or authorized private judge access, a description, and a public 1–3 minute YouTube/Vimeo demonstration. Judges need free access to a working build. English or an English translation is required. Existing work must explain substantial changes since September 29. Eligibility and third-party rights need owner review. Judging equally weights technological implementation, design, potential impact and idea quality; ties compare those criteria in order. [Official rules](https://youcam-api-skin-ai-ecommerce.devpost.com/rules), checked October 25, 2026.

## Schedule discrepancy
Rules specify November 3–20 judging, ending at 11:45 AM EST; the [schedule](https://youcam-api-skin-ai-ecommerce.devpost.com/details/dates) specifies November 9–20, ending at 5 PM EST. Preserve both. An eventual approved hosting plan should cover the later endpoint, November 20 at 3 PM Arizona, with any owner-approved buffer.

## Product evidence for judges
Our technical evidence is deterministic eligibility, valid catalog choices, bounded async VTO and human transaction control. Design evidence is a complete return journey with an accessible refund route. Impact evidence is correctly calculated simulated retained value, clearly labeled. Idea evidence is the post-purchase return decision and the ability to refuse unsuitable AI use.

A mock demo supports development but does not establish a qualifying live integration. Prior competitor rankings, participant counts, “9/10” estimates and winning predictions are source opinions, not official scores or validated benchmarks. Do not use them as submission claims.
